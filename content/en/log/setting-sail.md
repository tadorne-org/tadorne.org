This site opens today. Tadorne publishes open hardware and the engineering
around it — electronics, mechanics, firmware — and this log is where the
work will be documented as it happens: designs, measurements, and the
experiments that fail along the way. Everything published here is signed;
the [verify](/verify/) page explains how to check.

It opens with a project. **[Penon](https://penon.tadorne.org/)** sets out to put the
best wind-sensing technology there is into the hands of racing sailors —
as open source. Wind is the one instrument a racer trusts on every leg,
and the technology behind the best sensors deserves to be open: built,
understood, repaired and improved by the people who sail with it.

Penon starts now, at the workbench. Follow along here.
