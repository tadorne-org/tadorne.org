The Off du Rhum gathers the sailing crowd around the Route du Rhum. Beside
the race, there is room for another subject: open source and open hardware
built for the sea — instruments, sensors, autopilots, on-board electronics,
shared designs.

The slot is on Wednesday 28 October, from 2.30 pm to 4 pm — the venue is
still to be confirmed.

Tadorne is collecting initiatives to present there. If a project of yours
belongs on that list, fill in the form below. It is short, and open to
anyone building maritime open source or open hardware, whether the work is
mature or just starting out.
