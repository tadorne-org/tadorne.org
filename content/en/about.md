## The name

*Tadorne* is the French name of the common shelduck, *Tadorna tadorna* — a
large, unmistakable duck of Europe's Atlantic coasts, at home in the air, on
the water and on land. A fitting namesake for an engineering practice that
moves between domains rather than settling in one.

The palette is taken straight from its plumage: crème body, near-black
bottle-green head, a chestnut band across the chest, a vermilion bill.
Nothing was invented. The colours are on the page because they are on the
animal.

## What Tadorne publishes

Open hardware, complete enough to rebuild:

- **Schematics and PCB sources**, not exported images — the KiCad files, the
  netlist, the generation scripts.
- **Parametric mechanics** in OpenSCAD, with the STLs, so dimensions can be
  changed rather than reverse-engineered.
- **Firmware**, including the parts that are still a skeleton, marked as such.
- **Design documents** that record the trade-offs and the error budget, not
  just the final answer.
- **Engineering notes**, including experiments that failed.

Everything published here is signed with a single GPG key; the
[verify](/verify/) page explains how to check a signature, a release or a
git tag.
