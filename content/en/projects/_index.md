Each project ships as a complete set: schematic and PCB sources, parametric
mechanics, firmware, a design document explaining the trade-offs, and a
costed bill of material. Everything is signed with the Tadorne GPG key.

## Got a project to build?

Sensors, instrumentation, open hardware: if you need something these projects
don't cover, [get in touch](mailto:contact@tadorne.org?subject=Project).
Encrypted mail welcome, the key is on the [verify](/en/verify/) page.
